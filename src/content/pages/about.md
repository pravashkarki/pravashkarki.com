I am a product designer and technology consultant. I have spent twenty years making things people can use: print, then websites, then products, now an enterprise data platform at [Highstep Technologies](https://highstep.com), where I lead design and strategy.

I founded [LastDoor](https://lastdoorsolutions.com) in 2006 and have co-founded a few other companies since. I built an app most of Nepal once used to plan around power cuts, and I have taught human-computer interaction at university.

Lately I care about two things. How a person and several AI agents can work as one team without losing the plot; the rules I use for that are [public](https://github.com/pravashkarki/agent-operating-system). And mental health in Nepal, where I am training as a [psychosocial counselor](https://pcs.pravashkarki.com); [Mano](/products/) comes from that.

The full record, roles, education, certifications, is on the [CV page](/cv/).
