I am a product designer and technology consultant in Kathmandu, Nepal, with a little over twenty years of work behind me: graphic and web design first, then front-end development, SEO, brand strategy, and now enterprise product design. I work remotely for international clients, and I am training as a psychosocial counselor, which is where [Mano](/app/mano) and [Mano Atlas](https://pcs.pravashkarki.com) come from.

## Now

**Product Designer (Consultant), Highstep Technologies, New Jersey, USA** · since May 2025

Leading design and strategy for a new enterprise data-operations platform: product direction, user experience, and the day-to-day work of shipping it, delivered fully remotely from Kathmandu.

**Founder, LastDoor (Last Door Solutions Pvt Ltd)** · since 2006, incorporated 2011

A design-driven technology practice building enterprise-grade Drupal and WordPress sites for international clients, plus brand strategy, identity, AI automation and ongoing support. Day-to-day operations are led by a dedicated CEO. Through LastDoor and Upwork: 81 completed contracts, 3,400+ hours, a 5.0 rating across 71 reviews.

- **[Mano Atlas](https://pcs.pravashkarki.com)** (2026): bilingual mental-health education, 29 chapters from the CTEVT Psychosocial Counselor course.
- **[inkwell](https://github.com/pravashkarki/inkwell)**: open-source blog template for serious writers. Markdown in, clean HTML out, every post also served as raw Markdown for AI crawlers. This site runs on it.

- **Medibook**: digital health platform for booking and managing medical appointments.
- **WordPress themes** on the official directory since 2012; Drupal.org community member since 2010.
