## Mano · मनो (2026)

A small, free app for noticing how you feel, in Nepali and English. Tap the state that fits, get a warm response and a two-minute skill, and find verified Nepal helplines from every screen. No account, no internet, data never leaves the phone. Android and iOS. [Privacy and support](/app/mano/).

## Mano Atlas · मनो एट्लास (2026)

Free bilingual mental-health education: 29 chapters from the CTEVT Psychosocial Counselor course, written for students, community health workers, families and anyone learning. Diagrams, quick checks, a glossary. [pcs.pravashkarki.com](https://pcs.pravashkarki.com)

## inkwell (2026)

Open-source blog template for serious writers. Markdown files are the source of truth; every page is served as clean HTML for people and as raw Markdown for AI crawlers. This site runs on it. [github.com/pravashkarki/inkwell](https://github.com/pravashkarki/inkwell)

## BattiGayo (iOS, 2012 to 2018)

The load-shedding schedule app Nepalis at home and abroad used to plan around power cuts, published by Idealaya and featured in national media. Retired in 2018 when load shedding ended in Nepal. [The story](https://lastdoorsolutions.com/battigayo/)

## Medibook

Digital health platform for booking and managing medical appointments.

## Sampression WordPress themes (since 2012)
